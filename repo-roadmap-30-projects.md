## Best practice for building a strong repo

- Use real, live, or regularly updated data sources whenever possible.
- Keep each project focused on one business question.
- Include: data cleaning, analysis, visualization, insights, and conclusions.
- Add a README with: objective, data source, tools used, methodology, findings, and link to the dataset.
- Make sure the final output is readable and portfolio-ready.

## Suggested portfolio mix

A strong student portfolio could look like this:

- 6 SQL projects
- 6 Python projects
- 6 Excel/reporting projects
- 6 Power BI/Tableau dashboards
- 6 AI-assisted analytics reports

This gives a balanced portfolio without overwhelming the learner.

## Final note

The goal is not to collect 30 repositories just for the count. The goal is to build 10–15 strong projects that show practical knowledge, and then expand slowly as confidence grows.
